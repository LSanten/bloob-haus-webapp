Everything above the line is the top of the note. Six of its lines are written on the envelope: `to`, `from`, `timing` (the stamp), `intention`, `need` and `envelope_note`. Change one and the envelope changes.

This part is the paper inside. It holds only what you type, and a line ends
where you press Enter.

Try `timing: please-open-soon`, or your own words, like `timing: on your birthday`.
