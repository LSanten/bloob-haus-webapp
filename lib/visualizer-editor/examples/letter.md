Everything above the line is the outside of the letter: who it is for, who it is from, and when to open it. Change `to:` or `intention:` and the envelope changes.

This part is the paper inside. It holds only what you type, and a line ends
where you press Enter.

Try `timing: please-open-soon`, or your own words, like `timing: on your birthday`. Add a line of your own, like `mood: sunny`, and it is typed on the envelope too.
