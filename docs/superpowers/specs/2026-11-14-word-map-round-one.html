Word map, round one

A fresh agent built a word map from the public guide alone (making-visualizers.md, writing-notes-for-shapes.md, ui-principles.md), with no repo and no memory. Below: its note next to ours, then every difference, sorted. Comment on any row: agree, disagree, or say how it should be.

The differences

same

Groups, sizes, ties once, outlines from members, seeded layout

Headings for groups, +/- at the end, a tie written once, the group's outline computed around its words, the same note drawing the same map. It also caught the subtitle trap (a ## right under the title) and asks for a sentence in between, as writing-notes says.

The guide worked here. Nothing to change.

taste · theirs better?

A note is plain indented text, not note:

Theirs reads more like a real note in Obsidian: the sentence just sits under the word. It still keeps lines apart (plain text is the note, a child bullet is a relationship), which is writing-notes recommendation 3.

My lean: adopt theirs. Plain indented text is the note; note: still accepted when reading.

taste

A tie is - ~ rest, not - tied to: rest

Shorter to type, but it names only one kind of relationship. Ours names it, so grows from: or opposite of: can come later and just work. The guide said nothing about relationships (it shouldn't: that is the word map's own card).

My lean: keep tied to:. It reads as words, and it grows.

our card was wrong

The builder moved a tie the person had written

"guests ~ noise" was written under guests; after a round trip it sat under noise, because our rule says the builder writes a tie on the word that comes first in the file. A builder should never move what a person wrote.

Fix the card: the builder leaves an existing tie where it is; a new tie goes on the word you started from.

guide said nothing

Positions: percent of the board (@ 62,66) or pixels (@240,310)

Theirs survives a board of any size and fits "looks the same at every size". Ours follows garden, which draws on a fixed canvas. Nothing in the guide says which unit a position uses.

My lean: percent for boards that resize; the guide gets one line: say the unit, and prefer one that doesn't depend on screen size.

guide said nothing

How a builder hands the changed note back

Dragging a word rewrote the note, and the agent had to invent a bloob:change event to give it to the host. That is a real missing piece of the contract, not a detail.

Guide v1 gets an "interface" section: what parse receives (body and outside), how the closed view is asked for (renderClosed(outside)), how a builder hands back markdown, what goes in manifest.json, the token list.

guide said nothing

The closed view is decoration

It drew a little pattern of bars from the title. The ontology says a closed view should say what the thing is for ("tell what the need is first"), and the guide never passed that on.

Guide v1: a closed view shows what it is and what it is for, from the outside (title, an intention line), before any decoration.

taste

Groups are rounded rectangles; moving needs a "Move words" switch

Both follow from the guide (outlines from members; "nothing moves by accident"). The Thursday mockup has soft drawn loops and direct dragging.

Your call. I'd keep direct dragging on a board meant for moving things.

fine either way

No mermaid

Mermaid as a second way in lives on our card only; the guide never mentions it, and it shouldn't.

Nothing to change.
