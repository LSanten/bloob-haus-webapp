---
title: A note on paper
---
The beginning of a note shows, the rest is rolled up below. Click the paper to unroll it, and click again to roll it back up.

A note is a sketch or a thought in progress: less polished than a marble, more like a scribble on a napkin.

Write it like any markdown note:

- lists
- **bold** and *italic*
- [links](https://bloob.haus/)

> A quote sits on the paper too.

The longer the note, the longer it takes to unroll.
